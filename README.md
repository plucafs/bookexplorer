# bookexplorer
An epub reader and explorer for Android

## Features
- Read EPUB files scrolling through the paragraphs a là TikTok/Reels
- Feed mode: scroll through random paragraphs picked from your imported EPUBs
- Bookmark paragraphs and scroll through them
- Gestures
	- scroll through the ebooks list
	- tap to open
	- scroll back/forward to change paragraph
	- double tap to go back to the library
	- pinch inside to toggle the bookmark
	- tap the progress bar to open the Table of contents (TOC), scroll down to close it
- The reading position is saved, and you can stop to record it manually to move freely inside the book; is also saved when one tries to jump through chapters without blocking the recording of the reading position
